What this is

Ritual Tech invites you to compose encounters with the digital traces of your life: photographs, messages, notes, calendars, recordings.

The trace is everyday material. The score is a proposition. The encounter is the work.

Privacy

The machine never asks for, sees or stores your files. You explore your own material, on your own devices, in your own time.

Your cartridge settings are remembered only in this browser.

No outcome required

You don't need to make an artwork, keep anything, or show anyone. Documentation, objects and sharing are possible afterlives, never conditions.

The machine offers conditions for attention. It promises no meaning.

The machine dispenses the proposition. You create the encounter.
